About me

I like the unglamorous part of AI work: getting the data right, checking the numbers twice, and testing models the way they'll actually be used.

I studied computer science in Pune and spent my first working years in data and compliance, where a single wrong SQL join can quietly misreport thousands of refunds. That made me careful. Now I'm in Dublin, finishing an MSc at UCD and working on how LLMs should be governed when they make real decisions.

Off the keyboard I run shifts at Dublin Airport. It has taught me more about making calls under pressure than any course has.
